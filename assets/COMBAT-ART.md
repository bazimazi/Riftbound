## Rendering

Six action frames express anticipation, windup, release, follow-through, recovery and ready. Contact is brief, with more time spent on follow-through and settling. Simulation time advances these phases; menus freeze both the charge and its eventual release. Player input uses `Game.castSkill()` to reserve cooldown once, then release after a brief windup. Engine-triggered emergency skills remain immediate. Auto-attacks anticipate the end of their existing cooldown instead of firing extra shots. The escape skill's invulnerability starts at input.

The renderer locates transparent row gutters and isolates each connected actor silhouette. This removes neighboring cape/weapon fragments from nominal atlas cells without modifying the source files. Frame feet supply stable anchors; width-based scaling avoids shrinking heroes when their staff or hammer rises. Rook and Lumen have small scale adjustments to match their walk proportions. Small sprite rasters are prepared before the first run and cached with a 192-entry budget. The original four walk rows also receive gutter and silhouette isolation.

Projectiles retain their existing damage and collision paths. Rendered height, a short launch offset, fire lift, blade rotation, trails and shadows give them physical motion. Impacts use recovering sprite displacement and a cached silhouette highlight. Hero-specific effects stay grounded at their release origin. Decorative impact/muzzle particles stop being added near the effect budget; enemy warnings take precedence when trimming it. Reduced motion preserves necessary projectiles and threat cues while suppressing body animation, shaking and extra trails.
