# Wayfarer characters

`wayfarer-champions.png` supplies Rook and Lumen's transparent full-body art for the roster, portraits and menus. Generated with the built-in imagegen tool and copied locally; the original remains in the Codex generated-images folder. The atlas uses two equal cells, registered in `src/pixel-art.js`. Version 6.1 adds separate six-frame walk, attack and casting sequences, documented in [COMBAT-ART.md](COMBAT-ART.md).

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent game sprite atlas for Riftbound, a dark forest survivor game with polished readable high-resolution pixel art. Exactly TWO full-body characters in a SINGLE ROW with TWO equal square cells, each isolated centered inside its cell with generous empty padding, no overlap. LEFT character: Rook, a stout armored guardian, warm antique brass heavy armor, cream cape, massive square tower shield in his left hand and short rune stone war hammer in his right hand, glowing amber eyes under closed helmet, solid broad silhouette. RIGHT character: Lumen, an elegant moon archer, silver hair, midnight blue hooded cloak, turquoise crescent-shaped longbow, luminous cyan arrow, agile silhouette. Both three-quarter front facing down-right, game chibi proportions with large heads, full feet visible. Sophisticated detailed pixel clusters and clean strong contours, restrained internal detail readable at 80px tall, shaded by 3-4 palette levels, warm gold versus cool moonlight palettes. Actual transparent background, no environment, no ground shadows, no text, no labels, no cell dividers, no watermark. Landscape composition two equal cells. Frame characters consistently at same full body height.
